About us

Who we are

Anando Engineering Limited is a Lagos-based engineering and construction company. We build homes, commercial facilities and roads, and we manage projects for private developers, corporate clients, institutions and government agencies across Nigeria.

Alongside construction we install CCTV, security and solar systems, so a client can give one team the whole job, from the foundations to the cameras on the gate, and get clear reporting at every stage.
